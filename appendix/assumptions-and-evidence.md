## Contradictions and hypotheses to test

The combined waste estimate is not automatically arithmetically impossible: at 22.5% plates, 7.5% profiles and 8.5% combined, plates would be about (8.5-7.5)/(22.5-7.5) = 6.67% of input mass if these were the only classes and used identical definitions/periods. Those conditions are unverified. Demand a weighted material mix, not a forced reconciliation.

The reported annual capacity/monthly throughput discrepancy may be rounding or different measures; it is not evidence of dishonesty. Manual planning and automatic capability can coexist due to configuration, constraint handling or skill. Cross-project offcut checks can coexist with cost restrictions if transfers exist, but no transfer mechanism is established. Treat each as a testable uncertainty.

Hidden root-cause hypotheses: purchased stock-size mismatch; inaccessible/off-register remnants; revision rework; expired releases; schedule fragmentation; project holds; missing consumption events; operator incentives that optimise local yield; and unreliable loss classification. None is asserted as a proven cause.
